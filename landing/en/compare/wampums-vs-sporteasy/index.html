Wampums vs Sporteasy: Scouting-Specific vs General Sports Management

Sporteasy is a well-regarded platform for managing sports teams — scheduling matches, tracking rosters, and communicating with players and parents. But running a scout group is not the same as running a hockey team. Scouts have badge progressions, overnight camps, bilingual families, permission slips, medication tracking, and a volunteer leadership structure that looks nothing like a coaching staff. This comparison explains exactly where a general sports management app falls short for scout and youth groups, and why Wampums — built from the ground up for this specific use case — is a better fit for Canadian organizations.

See all features

Native Bilingual Support for Canadian Families

Canada's scout movement is bilingual. A single group often includes families where some parents are most comfortable in English and others in French. SportEasy is a French company, and its app exists in French, English and five other languages; the difference lies in what a scout unit needs.

Wampums stores a language preference for every user. When a French-speaking parent logs in, they see the entire application in French — navigation, notifications, forms, and reports. When an English-speaking leader logs in, they see everything in English. There is no manual toggling, no separate French communication workflow, and no risk of sending English-only reminders to Francophone families. The bilingual experience is automatic and complete.

Badge and Achievement Tracking Built In

Badge progression is central to the scouting experience. From Beaver challenges to the Scouts Canada Canadian Path, every youth has a set of requirements to complete over weeks, months, or years. SportEasy is built around matches and training; we found no badge tracking in it.

Wampums provides structured badge and achievement records for each participant. Leaders check off requirements from a predefined list; the platform maintains a completion history with dates and the name of the verifying leader. Parents can view their child's progress in their own language. When a scout earns a badge, the record is permanent and travels with them through leadership transitions. No spreadsheets, no binders — just a clear, structured record in the platform.

Scout-Specific Workflows: Permission Slips, Medications, and Overnight Camps

A typical scout season includes day hikes, overnight camps, and special events — each requiring parent consent, emergency contacts, and often medication information. SportEasy’s events are built around games, training and attendance replies; we found no health form workflow or medication log in it.

Wampums includes purpose-built workflows for each of these scout realities. The form builder handles consent forms, medical declarations, and emergency contact collection with e-signature support. The medications module tracks what each participant is taking, dosage schedules, and dispensing records — all with the detail a camp director needs and all in compliance with the parent's language preference. These are not features bolted onto a sports platform; they are native to how Wampums was designed.

Offline Mode for Camps and Remote Locations

Scout meetings happen in community halls with unreliable WiFi. Camps happen in provincial parks with no cell signal. An app that needs a live server connection stops helping there.

Wampums is built offline-first. Critical data — participant rosters, medical information, medication schedules, attendance sheets, and carpool assignments — is cached locally on the leader's device. Changes made without internet are queued and sync automatically when connectivity returns. A leader at a remote campsite can take attendance, log a medication dispensing, and check emergency contacts without ever needing a signal. This is not an afterthought feature; it is a core architectural decision that shapes how the entire platform works.

Canadian Data Privacy

Scout groups collect sensitive data about children — medical information, emergency contacts, photos, and behavioral notes. Canadian organizations are subject to the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, in some provinces, additional provincial privacy legislation. Sending it outside Quebec is not forbidden, but Quebec's Law 25 requires an organization to assess the privacy risks first, and that is work volunteer leaders rarely have time for.

Wampums keeps each unit's database and files in Quebec, at OVHcloud's Beauharnois data centre. Everything travels over HTTPS, backups are encrypted, each unit sees only its own records, and roles decide who can open a health form. When a family asks, a leader can permanently erase a child's record. Emails go out through Brevo, which receives each recipient's address and the message, and the optional AI-assisted writing receives only the text a leader submits.

Cost and Value for Volunteer-Led Organizations

SportEasy offers a free team app and paid offers for teams and clubs, priced on its site. For a scout group that needs attendance tracking, badge management, parent communication, permission slips, finance, medications, and carpool coordination, the cost of trying to piece together these workflows in a sports-focused platform — or paying for multiple tools — adds up quickly. Volunteer organizations with tight budgets need tools that deliver value without requiring budget committee approval every year.

A single unit uses Wampums free until at least 2028. For an organization opening several units (five or more, say), we agree on a competitive monthly price together. All modules — attendance, badges, finance, forms, medications, carpool and communications — are included, with no per-module upsell.

Questions about Wampums vs Sporteasy

Does Sporteasy support badge tracking for scouts?

No. Sporteasy is designed for sports teams and does not include badge or achievement tracking. Wampums tracks badges with levels and approval steps, configured to your unit's program.

Is Sporteasy available in French for Canadian groups?

Yes: SportEasy is a French company and its app exists in French, English and several other languages. Wampums also lets each family read messages and forms in its own language.

Does Wampums store data in Canada?

Yes. Each unit's database and files are hosted in Quebec, at OVHcloud's Beauharnois data centre. Emails go out through Brevo, which receives each recipient's address and the message, and the optional AI-assisted writing receives only the text a leader submits.

Can Wampums handle permission slips and medical forms?

Yes. Wampums includes a built-in form builder that handles permission slips, medical information, emergency contacts, and custom forms — with e-signature support and automatic tracking of who has and hasn't responded.
