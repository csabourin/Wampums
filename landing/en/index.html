Built bilingual

Write once. Every family reads it in their language.

Half your families speak French, half speak English. In Wampums each person picks a language, and screens, reminders and forms follow.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do we get started?

Request a demo and we'll set up your unit. You can import your existing participants, guardians and leaders from CSV files and be running within a day.

How much does Wampums cost?

Wampums is free for scout groups during the early-access period. Write to us for details on future pricing.

Is our data secure?

Yes. Everything travels over HTTPS, backups are encrypted, and each person only sees what their role allows. Your unit's data is kept separate from every other unit's and is never shared with third parties.

Can we work in English and French?

Yes. Every screen, form and notification exists in both languages, and each person chooses their own. A unit can also add Ukrainian, Italian or Indonesian.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Wampums is a web app that installs from the browser on any phone, tablet or computer, with no app store needed. Attendance and points keep working offline and sync when you're back in range.

Can we customize forms and fields?

Yes. The form builder lets you create registration forms, health forms and permission slips with conditional questions and labels in both languages.

Can we import our existing data?

Yes. Participants, guardians and users can be imported from CSV files, and we can help you move data over from another system.

Does it fit groups other than Scouts?

Yes. Roles, permissions, forms and even the names of your subgroups (patrols, dens, lodges, sixes) are set by each unit, so Guides, Cadets and other youth groups use it too.
