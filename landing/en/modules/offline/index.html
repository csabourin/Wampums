Scout App Offline Mode — Attendance, Points, Medication and Badges Without Internet

Three bars of LTE at the trailhead, one bar by the dining hall, and nothing whatsoever at the campfire circle. Without a network, Wampums lets you take attendance, award points, record medication doses and record badges: those changes wait on the phone and go out as soon as the signal returns. The rest, such as incidents or messages to parents, needs a connection.

See all features

Optimistic Updates — Instant UI Response

When you mark a scout present or record a dose, the change appears on screen right away, without waiting for the server. On a weak connection or none at all, nothing blocks your work.

In the background, the change waits on the phone and goes out as soon as the connection returns. A banner shows that you are offline and how many changes are still waiting to be sent.

Offline Attendance — Full Roster Available Locally

Your unit's youth list stays on your phone for about a week each time you open Wampums with a connection. Mark present or absent with one tap, without a network. Buttons are large and well spaced, for cold fingers and dark tents alike.

Several leaders can take attendance at the same time, each from their own phone, one subgroup each for instance. If two leaders mark the same youth for the same date, the last attendance sent is the one that counts.

Offline Badge and Points Tracking

At camp, youth complete badge requirements all day — orienteering in the morning, outdoor cooking in the afternoon, first aid in the evening. Wampums lets you record badges the moment the activity ends, even without a network. If two leaders record the same level, the server counts it once.

Points work offline too: each batch awarded without a network waits on the phone and is sent once when the signal returns, so a total added from several phones is never counted twice. Without a network, note them down and enter them when the signal returns.

Offline Medication Dispensing

If the camp was prepared before leaving, the dose schedule and the medications to give are on the phone, and each dose is recorded without a network with the time it was given. Reminder notifications go through the server and need a connection, so without a network, check the schedule in the app.

Doses recorded without a network go out as soon as the signal returns. Until then, other leaders do not see them, so agree on who gives which dose. If two phones marked the same dose, the second is refused when the network returns, and Wampums says so.

Mutation Queue — Every Change Tracked

Changes made without a network are kept in a queue on your phone. It survives closing the app and restarting the phone, and each change keeps the time it was made.

When the connection returns, sending starts on its own. If the server refuses a change, such as a dose already given or access removed in the meantime, Wampums removes it from the queue and tells you which one, instead of announcing that everything was sent.

Camp Mode — Prepared Before You Leave, Kept for at Least 10 Days

Before leaving, with a connection, each leader prepares their phone, from the activity or with “Prepare for Offline”. Wampums downloads, for every date of the camp, the youth and group lists, attendance, medications and the dose schedule, badges and carpools, and keeps them for at least 10 days.

Full health forms and parents' contact details are not part of this bundle, so print the health and contact report before you go. Prepared data stays on the phone; protect it with a passcode.

Questions about offline mode

How long can the app run without internet?

Without preparation, the youth list, groups and upcoming activities stay on the phone for about a week, as long as you opened Wampums with a connection. A camp prepared before leaving keeps its data for at least 10 days.

What features work offline?

Without a network, you can read what is already on the phone, take attendance, award points, record doses and medications received, and record badges. Incidents, carpools, messages to parents, invitations and changes to the unit need a connection.

How does sync work when connectivity returns?

Each change made without a network waits in a queue on the phone. As soon as the signal returns, sending starts on its own. A change the server refuses, such as a dose already given, is removed from the queue and reported, rather than counted as sent.

What is camp mode and how do I activate it?

Camp mode keeps on the phone, for at least 10 days, what the team needs for the camp's dates: the youth list, attendance, medications and the dose schedule, badges and carpools. Each leader prepares their own phone before leaving, with a connection, from the activity or with “Prepare for Offline”. Health forms and parents' contact details are not included, so print the health and contact report before you go.
