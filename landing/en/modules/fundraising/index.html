Scout Fundraiser Management — From Cookie Sales to Annual Campaigns

Scout fundraising runs on enthusiasm, not spreadsheets — but at some point someone has to track who sold what, how much came in, and whether you hit the target. Wampums replaces the paper tally sheets and disconnected Google Docs with a structured fundraiser module that covers everything from creating the campaign to archiving the final report, and feeds the results directly into your financial dashboard.

See all features

Fundraiser Creation — Name, Goal, Dates, Type

Creating a fundraiser takes under two minutes: a name, a goal, start and end dates, and the type of campaign — for example a fixed-price sale, with its unit price and what is being sold.

The fundraiser is visible to authorized leaders right away, ready for its first sales.

Participant Tracking — Assign Scouts and Track Sales per Person

Each youth has a line on the fundraiser's sales sheet: what they sold and what they handed in. Leaders keep it up to date through the campaign.

Totals add up as sales are entered, which quickly shows who needs a hand or some encouragement.

Printable Sales Sheets

For a door-to-door sale, print the sales sheets before heading out and enter the results when you are back.

Several fundraisers can run at the same time — a fall campaign, a holiday sale, a spring event — and each keeps its own sales.

Progress Dashboard — Total vs. Target

The fundraiser's screen shows total sales next to the goal, with no paper sheets to add up.

The breakdown by youth sits right below: who sold what, and who has not handed anything in yet. It is the screen the fundraising lead keeps open throughout the campaign.

Archive Completed Fundraisers

When a fundraiser ends, archive it: it leaves the active list and stays available, with all its figures.

You can pull up last year's sale to compare it with this year's, or to report on it to your council.

Integration with Revenue Dashboard

Each fundraiser's revenue shows in the unit's revenue view, next to fees and other revenue, with no double entry.

A fundraiser can also be tied to a budget category, and the financial report includes it when you print or export it for the parents' meeting or the district.

Questions about fundraiser management

Can I run multiple fundraisers simultaneously?

Yes. Several fundraisers can run at the same time — a fall campaign, a holiday sale, a spring event — and each keeps its own sales.

How do I track per-participant sales?

Each youth has a line on the fundraiser's sales sheet: what they sold and what they handed in, kept up to date by leaders through the campaign.

Does fundraiser data feed into financial reports?

Yes. Each fundraiser's revenue shows in the unit's revenue view, next to fees, with no double entry.

Can parents see their child's fundraiser progress?

Yes. Parents see their own child's sales in their space, but not other families'.
