Scout Medication Tracking — Every Dose Planned, Every Dose Recorded

When a youth takes daily medication or carries an EpiPen, the team needs to know what to give, to whom and when. Wampums brings together the dose schedule, the log of what was given and the medications received from parents, from the consent form to a camp with no network. It is an aid for the team: giving medication and checking it remain the responsibility of the adults your unit designates.

See all features

Medication Profile per Participant

The health form parents fill in gathers medications, allergies, emergency contacts and whatever else your form asks. The section's health report flags who carries an EpiPen, so the team knows who has particular needs before the youth arrive.

A youth can have several medications, each with its dosage, route, frequency (set times, times of day or an interval) and instructions such as "take with food".

Dose Schedule and Reminders

Set up each medication's schedule, and upcoming doses show in Wampums. During an activity in progress, fifteen minutes before a dose, Wampums sends a notification to leaders who can see medications and have accepted notifications. It names the youth and the medication.

This reminder is an aid, not a guarantee. It needs a connection, and a phone that is off, flat, silenced or out of range will not receive it. At camp, check the dose schedule in the app and keep the checking routine your unit has set.

Dispensing Log — Who Gave What, When, and What Dose

Every dose given is recorded with the time, the leader who gave it, the medication and the dose, and a witness's name if you note one. A dose already marked as given cannot be marked again. If a parent or physician asks what was given and when, the answer is in the youth's history.

A dose can also be marked as missed or cancelled, so the log also shows what did not happen. The medication report exports to CSV or prints as PDF.

Camp Mode — Prepared Before You Leave, Usable Without a Network

Remote camps often have no reliable cellular or Wi-Fi coverage. Before leaving, with a connection, a leader prepares the camp in Wampums: the medications to give, the dose schedule and the medications received then stay on their phone for at least 10 days. Full health forms are not part of this bundle, so print the section's health report before you go.

Doses recorded without a network wait on the phone and are sent as soon as the connection returns, with the time they were given. Until then, other leaders do not see them, so agree on who gives which dose. If two phones marked the same dose, the second is refused when the network returns, and Wampums says so.

Parental Consent and Health Form Integration

Parents fill in, online and before the activity, the health form and whatever forms your unit asks for. Leaders see which youth have handed in their forms and which are still outstanding.

Forms exist in English and French, and each parent fills in theirs in their language. They are kept with the youth's record, rather than in a stack of paper that goes missing before camp.

How Health Data Is Handled

Health and medication data is among the most sensitive personal information a youth organization collects. Wampums stores health records in Quebec, sends them only over HTTPS, encrypts its backups and limits access by each person's role. During a prepared camp, part of this data is also kept on the leaders' phones: protect them with a passcode, and sign out of a shared device.

When a family asks, a leader can permanently erase a child's record, health data included.

Questions about medication tracking

How are medication details secured?

Medication data travels over HTTPS and is stored in Quebec, at OVHcloud's Beauharnois data centre, with encrypted backups. Emails go out through Brevo, which receives each recipient's address and the message, and the optional AI-assisted writing receives only the text a leader submits. The other providers are listed in the privacy policy. Access is restricted by role: only authorized leaders and administrators can open a youth's health form. During a prepared camp, medications and the dose schedule are also kept on the leaders' phones.

Can multiple leaders access medication records during a camp?

Yes. Each leader opens records from their own phone, as their role's permissions allow. A dose recorded without a network shows up for the others only once it is sent, so agree on who gives which dose.

Does the medication module work offline at camp?

Yes, if the camp was prepared before leaving, with a connection: medications, the dose schedule and the medications received then stay on the phone for at least 10 days. Doses recorded without a network are sent when the connection returns. Reminder notifications, though, need a connection.

How do parents update their child's medication information?

Parents update their child's health form from their own space at any time. Wampums does not alert the team to each change, so review the forms before a camp, for example in the health report.
