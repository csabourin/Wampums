Scout Carpool Coordination — Know Who's Driving Your Kids

Coordinating carpools for scout activities is one of those tasks that sounds simple but routinely turns into a last-minute scramble. Who offered to drive this week? How many seats do they have? Which kids still need a ride home? Is anyone going both ways? The answers to these questions are usually scattered across a group chat, someone's memory, and a half-complete spreadsheet. Wampums replaces all of that with a structured, transparent carpool coordination module where parents offer rides, parents assign their own children, and everyone — parents and leaders — knows exactly who is in which vehicle before the event starts.

See all features

Parent Ride Offers — Simple and Structured

Parents who can drive other scouts create a ride offer from their own space: the vehicle (make and colour), the number of seats, whether it covers the trip there, back or both, and a note if needed. Every offer for an activity is gathered in one view.

Creating an offer takes under a minute, even between two meetings. The driving parent sees who rides with them and can change or withdraw the offer if plans change; the families of youth already placed then receive an email about it.

Parents Place Their Own Children

The activity's carpool screen lists every offer with its driver, free seats and direction. Each parent places their own child in a vehicle with room, for one direction or both. A full vehicle takes no more passengers.

Everyone sees who rides with whom, so no leader has to dispatch rides by text message. For siblings or neighbours to ride together, families agree among themselves and choose the same vehicle.

Quick-Access Carpool Modal — Vehicles and Seats at a Glance

At the event, leaders need fast access to carpool information without navigating through multiple screens. Wampums provides a quick-access carpool window that opens from the activity with a single tap. It shows every vehicle for the activity, its driver, the free and taken seats, and the passengers, for both directions.

It is designed for the moment a parent asks "is there room in a car for my child today?". When the camp was prepared before leaving, it reads from the phone even without a connection.

Activity-Specific Carpools — Each Event Stands Alone

Scout groups run activities with very different carpool requirements. A Tuesday evening meeting in town may need no carpool coordination at all, while a Saturday hike 40 kilometres away requires careful seat allocation and precise pickup logistics. Wampums treats each activity's carpool as independent — ride offers, assignments, and notifications are scoped to the specific activity, not carried over from previous events or blended across concurrent activities.

For multi-day events like camps or jamborees, the trip there and the trip back are coordinated separately. A parent who offered seats for the drive to camp but cannot drive home is shown exactly that.

Who Rides With Whom, for Every Family

Once their child is placed, each family sees on its own dashboard, in its own language, the car, the driver and the other passengers.

When a driver withdraws an offer, the families of the youth placed in that car receive an email, so they can find another ride before the activity.

Offline Reading — Carpools Saved With the Prepared Camp

Scout activities regularly happen in locations where internet access is unavailable. An outdoor hiking trail, a provincial park campsite, or a remote sports facility may have no cell signal at all. At these locations, a leader who needs to verify that every participant has a ride home — or a parent who needs to know which car to look for at pickup — cannot rely on a connected web application.

When a leader prepares the camp before leaving, Wampums saves carpool offers and assignments on their phone, so who rides with whom can be read without a data connection. Changes to carpools need a connection: make them before leaving, or once back in range.

Questions about scout carpool coordination

Can parents see who their child is riding with?

Yes. Once carpool assignments are set for an activity, parents can view the driver name and the other passengers in the vehicle from their dashboard in their preferred language. Parents can access this information at any time through their parent dashboard.

Can a parent offer to drive for the trip to the activity but not back?

Yes. Wampums supports three trip direction options when a parent submits a carpool offer: to the activity only, from the activity only, or both directions. Parents can assign their own children separately for each direction based on availability.

Does the carpool module work offline at the event location?

Yes, for reading. When a leader prepares the camp before leaving, carpool offers and assignments are saved on their phone and can be read without internet, which helps at outdoor venues and camps where connectivity is unreliable. Changes to carpools need a connection.

Can carpool arrangements be set up for recurring meetings?

No. Each activity has its own carpool, so a parent who drives every week offers seats for each activity. Nothing carries over from one activity to the next.
