# Camp permit request: checklist and header

Issue #1416, first slice of the camp plan document and approval (#1320, tracking #1316). The camp
permit asks the team to settle a list of requirements. Almost all of them already live in Wampums;
this screen reads each one where it lives and says, line by line, how it stands. Nothing is copied.

## Screen

**`/camp-permit/:meetingId`** ("Camp permit request"; `meetings.view`, unit-wide scope), opened from
each camp of `/camp-plans` and from the camp overview (`/camp-readiness/:id`). `/camp-plan/:id` stays
the screen of the written sections, one of the checklist's sources. Slices 3 and 4 add the document and
the approvals to this screen.

It shows the plan's **header** (unit, scout group, district, age group, coeducation, dates, number of
days and of youth), a count of lines in each state, then the lines grouped as `config/camp_permit.json`
`GROUPS` lists them. Each line says its state in words (*complete*, *to complete*, *not verified*;
the border style repeats it, never colour alone), what is missing, and links to the screen that
completes it when the person may open that screen. A blocking gap (no verified first aider) is
introduced by the word "Blocking:".

## Endpoint

`GET /api/v1/camp-plans/camps/:meetingId/permit` (`meetings.view`, unit-wide scope; `no-store`),
`services/campPermit.js`. Another unit's camp, or a meeting that is not a camp, answers 404
`camp_permit_camp_not_found`. Rights a line's source needs come from the authorization engine:

| Lines | Right | Without it |
|---|---|---|
| Youth list, team, health forms, allergies; the header's number of youth | `participants.view` | `no_access`, `youth_count: null` |
| Transportation | `carpools.view` | `no_access` |
| Camp evaluation | `camp_reviews.view` | `no_access` |

## Rules

- **Nothing is complete by default**, and nothing says a camp is compliant. An empty source is "to
  complete"; a source Wampums lacks (menu #1260, letter to parents #1321, approvals #1419) or cannot
  check (transportation, free-text material, swimming supervision) is
  "not verified".
- **States and counts only.** The answer names no youth and carries no health information. The camp
  lists are read with no right to names, certifications or allergies (`ROSTER_RIGHTS`).
- A line the person may not verify reads `no_access`, with no detail and no link.
- Every reason has a `camp_permit_reason_<reason>` text (`REASONS`, checked by
  `test/spa/TranslationCoverage.test.js`).
- **Header.** The scout group is the unit's group membership (`organization_local_groups`), the district
  `organization_info.district`, the age group the unit's branch (`organizations.program_section`), and
  **coeducation** `organization_info.coeducation` (`mixed`, `girls`, `boys`, or empty), chosen under Unit
  settings and validated by `PATCH /api/v1/organizations/settings/organization-info`.
- The schedule is complete when every day of the camp has a line **and a title** (the plan uses the title as
  the day's narrative heading).
- The evaluation is "not verified" until the camp is over, then "to complete" until its review is closed.

## Schedule lines (#1417)

The camp schedule (`CampSchedule`, `/api/v1/yearly-planner/meetings/:id/schedule`) gives each line:

- **Logistic notes**, `year_plan_meeting_activities.description` (plain text, at most 4000 characters).
- **Members in charge**, `year_plan_meeting_activity_leaders` (migration 081), in the order chosen, at most
  ten, through `leader_ids` on `POST /meetings/:id/activities` and `PATCH /meeting-activities/:id`
  (`services/scheduleLeaders.js`). Only active members of the unit whose roles reach beyond their own
  children may be newly named (the camp team's candidates); anyone else is 400 `schedule_leaders_invalid`.
  A member who has left stays named, marked `active_member: false`; they may be kept or removed, never
  newly added. The editor offers checkboxes, the camp's team (`team_user_ids`) first. The free-text
  `responsable` stays for a person without an account. Copying or reusing a camp does not copy who led
  what: people change from year to year.
- **Concurrent edits.** The editor sends the `updated_at` it loaded as `expected_updated_at`; a line changed
  since is refused with 409 `schedule_line_changed` and the editor reloads it. Callers that omit the field
  are unchanged.
- Meals still have no menu to point to: that link comes with the menus (#1260).

## Frozen versions and the document (#1418)

**Submitting** (`POST /api/v1/camp-plans/camps/:id/versions`; `meetings.manage` and `participants.view`,
unit-wide; never a demo account) locks the camp, refuses a cancelled one (409 `camp_plan_camp_cancelled`),
and stores a new `camp_plan_versions` row (migration 082) in one transaction:

- `snapshot`: everything the document holds, read where it lives (`services/campPlanVersions.js`,
  `buildPlanSnapshot`): header and checklist, written sections **with the template texts then in force**,
  bad-weather games, schedule with members in charge and notes, site and emergency facts, team with
  verified qualifications, volunteers **counted by type of shift, never named** (names are for managers of
  volunteers only), youth, group material. **No health information.**
- `snapshot_sha256`, whose first eight characters, in capitals and groups of four, are the version's
  **verification code** ("2CEC 53A4"). It is printed on the PDF's first page and shown in Wampums: people
  check that a copy (attached to the permit request, emailed) is this version by comparing two short codes. "SHA-256"
  means nothing to most people, so the screen never leads with it.
- `pdf`, generated by the server from the snapshot alone (`services/campPlanPdf.js`, PDFKit), and
  `pdf_sha256`, the file's full fingerprint, shown only under "Technical details" for whoever needs to check
  a file byte for byte. The version's number, date, verification code and the unit's language (its default
  email language, `en` or `fr`) are printed in it.
- `pdf_health` / `pdf_health_sha256`: the same document whose allergies section names youth and adults,
  generated only when there is something to name.
- `health_sha256`: a fingerprint of the allergy information as submitted, never the information itself.
  A reader holding `camp_allergies.view` is told (`allergies` in `changed_since_latest`) when it changed
  since the latest version; nobody else is, since that change is itself health information.
- **Erasure.** The snapshot keeps each youth's `participant_id` (never printed). When a family's erasure
  removes their child (`services/erasure.js` → `redactParticipantFromVersions`), every version naming the
  child is rewritten without them: snapshot and PDF regenerated, the copy naming allergies deleted (it cannot
  be rebuilt without the erased information), `redacted_at` set. The verification code changes, so older
  copies no longer match, and the screen says why.
- The version still `submitted` becomes `superseded`. Slice 4 adds `changes_requested` and `approved`.

A version never changes afterwards: editing the camp, its site or the unit's templates leaves it as
submitted. `GET …/versions` lists them (newest first, no bytes) with `changed_since_latest`: the parts of
the current plan (`SNAPSHOT_PARTS`) that differ from the latest version, compared with sorted keys since
JSONB does not keep key order. `GET …/versions/:n/pdf` sends the document (`no-store`, `X-Content-SHA256`):
the copy naming allergies only to holders of `camp_allergies.view`, otherwise the plain copy, whose
allergies section says where to find them. Each listed `pdf_sha256` is the fingerprint of the copy that
reader downloads. Reading versions takes `meetings.view` and `participants.view` (they name youth) **and**
`meetings.manage` or `volunteers.manage` (they show team qualifications, as the camp lists require). The service worker never caches these addresses (`isPersonalProfileRequest`).

**The PDF is accessible** (PDF/UA): a structure tree (`Document`, `Sect`, `H1`–`H3`, `P`, `Table` with
`TH` cells naming their scope), the document language (`fr-CA` / `en-CA`), its title shown in the window,
and embedded fonts carrying accents (DejaVu Sans, `dejavu-fonts-ttf`). Every missing part is printed
"to complete", never left out. Its texts are `camp_plan_pdf_*` keys and the screen's own, read from
`lang/<language>.json` on the server (`test/camp-plan-pdf.test.js` checks each exists in both languages).

On `/camp-permit/:id`, "Submitted versions" (with `participants.view`) offers **Submit for approval**
(confirmed first), lists each version with its state, who submitted it and when, its verification code (the full
fingerprint folded under "Technical details") and a download button, and says what changed since the latest. The client downloads through
`API.getFile()` (`spa/api/api-core.js`), never a bare `fetch`.
