# Security Policy

Security and privacy are important to Wampums, especially because the application may process personal information about youth participants, their parents or guardians, and other sensitive records.

We appreciate responsible reports from security researchers, developers, and members of the community.

## Supported Versions

Security fixes are provided for the current production version of Wampums. Older releases and unofficial copies are not actively supported.

| Version or deployment | Supported |
| --- | --- |
| Current production version | ✅ |
| Previous versions | ❌ |
| Unofficial forks or modified deployments | ❌ |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public GitHub issues, discussions, or other public forums before they have been investigated.**

Report security concerns privately by emailing **info@wampums.app** with the subject line **`[SECURITY] Vulnerability report – Wampums`**.

If possible, include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- The affected feature, page, API endpoint, or component.
- Clear steps to reproduce the issue, including any prerequisites.
- A proof of concept or screenshots, if relevant, using only test or redacted data.
- Any suggested mitigations or fixes.
- A safe way to contact you for follow-up questions.

**Do not include real participant, parent or guardian records, health information, passwords, access tokens, or other confidential data in your report.** If sensitive information is essential to demonstrate an issue, contact us first so we can agree on an appropriate way to share it securely.

### What to Expect

- **Acknowledgement:** We aim to acknowledge your report within **5 business days**.
- **Assessment:** We will review the report, attempt to reproduce the issue, and assess its severity and potential impact.
- **Updates:** For confirmed issues under active investigation, we aim to provide a status update at least every **14 days**, where practical.
- **Resolution:** Confirmed vulnerabilities will be prioritized according to their severity and risk. Fix timelines may vary depending on complexity and impact.
- **Outcome:** If the report is accepted, we will coordinate any appropriate disclosure after a fix or mitigation is available. If it is declined or cannot be reproduced, we will explain the decision where possible.

### Responsible Disclosure

To help protect Wampums users while a report is investigated, please:

- Avoid accessing, modifying, downloading, or disclosing anyone else's personal information.
- Use test accounts and synthetic data wherever possible.
- Avoid disrupting the service, degrading its availability, or performing destructive testing.
- Do not attempt social engineering, phishing, or attacks against other users.
- Give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address a reported vulnerability before publishing technical details.

We appreciate your efforts to help keep Wampums and its community safe.
